From Noob to Pro: Roblox Rivals Essentials

By RivalsGuide

To go from "Noob" to "Pro" in Roblox Rivals, you need to master three things: Aim, Reaction Speed, and Movement. In this fast-paced arena, Aim is about landing headshots to end duels instantly. Reaction Speed keeps you alive when an opponent surprises you, allowing you to flick and fire back. Finally, Movement is the "secret sauce"—using slides and dashes makes you a hard target to hit. Combine these fundamentals with map knowledge, and you’ll stop hiding and start dominating the leaderboard.
